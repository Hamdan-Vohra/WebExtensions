We have mentioned about the extensions that what they are and how they are being used.
In this file, we will deal with technical terms and what the web exstensions includes.

To show popup below the extension icon, we would show thorugh popup.html, popup.css for styling and popup.js for script.
The name of the files could be dynamic, but we have to configure these files in manifest.json in 'actions' key

Then, we have an option to get access to the opened TAB DOM content, thorugh 'content_scripts' key, where we mention two things
'files' array and 'matches' urls array
Important thing about content scripts is that they have power to access the DOMContent, also their logs will be shown in the tab console
while popup.js logs will be shown in the console of that popup
